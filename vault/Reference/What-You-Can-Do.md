# What Your AI Assistant Can Do

This document covers the main use cases for your AI assistant, from everyday tasks to advanced workflows.

---

## Email Management

Your AI can help you stay on top of email without the overwhelm.

**Common requests:**
- "Scan my inbox and summarize what needs attention"
- "Find all emails from [company/person] in the last month"
- "Draft a response to [person] about [topic]"
- "Create a follow-up email for the meeting we had yesterday"

**How it works:**
- AI reads your inbox via Gmail integration
- Summarizes and categorizes messages
- Drafts responses (always as drafts - you review before sending)
- Helps with professional tone and completeness

---

## Voice Memo Ingestion

Capture thoughts on the go, let AI turn them into structured notes.

**Common requests:**
- "Transcribe my latest voice memo"
- "I recorded some thoughts on my walk - turn them into action items"
- "Transcribe this meeting recording and pull out key decisions"

**How it works:**
- Point AI to your audio file
- Gemini-powered transcription
- AI can then process the transcript: extract tasks, summarize, or organize

**Tip:** Record quick voice memos when ideas strike. Process them later with AI help.

## Research & Analysis

Deep research on any topic, synthesized for decision-making.

**Common requests:**
- "Research [topic] and give me a summary"
- "Compare these three options for [decision]"
- "What are the pros and cons of [approach]?"
- "Find recent developments in [industry/technology]"

**Advanced workflow:**
1. Start with deep research using tools like Perplexity
2. Feed the research output to your AI
3. Ask for synthesis, recommendations, or action items

**Example:**
> "I ran a Perplexity deep research on electric vehicle charging infrastructure.
> Here's the output: [paste]. Summarize the key insights and recommend
> next steps for my business."

## Tutoring & Education

Generate practice materials, evaluate learning, identify gaps.

**Common requests:**
- "Create a practice test on [subject] at [difficulty level]"
- "Grade this test and identify weak areas"
- "Generate study notes for [topic]"
- "Create flashcards for [subject]"
- "What concepts from this test need more work?"

**Advanced workflow:**
1. Generate practice test aligned to curriculum
2. Student completes test
3. AI evaluates responses and identifies gaps
4. AI generates targeted remediation exercises
5. Repeat until mastery

**Tip:** Great for parents helping kids study, or for your own learning.

## Health & Wellness Optimization

Use AI to analyze health data and optimize protocols.

**Common requests:**
- "Review my supplement stack for interactions"
- "Analyze these blood work results"
- "Help me design an exercise protocol for [goal]"
- "What does this lab value mean and is it optimal?"
- "Research the latest on [health topic]"

**Important:** AI provides information for discussion with healthcare providers. Not medical advice.

**Workflow example:**
1. Upload blood work results
2. AI extracts and organizes values
3. Compare to optimal ranges (not just "normal")
4. Identify areas for optimization
5. Research evidence-based interventions
6. Discuss with your doctor

## Legal Research & Analysis

AI as a legal research assistant for contracts, agreements, and compliance.

**Common requests:**
- "Review this contract and flag concerning terms"
- "What are the key obligations in this agreement?"
- "Research [legal topic] in [jurisdiction]"
- "Compare these two contract versions"
- "What questions should I ask my lawyer about this?"

**Use cases:**
- **M&A transactions** - Due diligence, document review, term analysis
- **Employment agreements** - Review offer letters, non-competes, severance
- **Commercial contracts** - Vendor agreements, service contracts, NDAs
- **Compliance** - Research regulatory requirements

**Important:** AI helps with research and analysis. Always consult qualified legal counsel for decisions.

**Workflow example:**
1. Feed contract to AI
2. AI identifies key terms, obligations, risks
3. Generate list of questions for your lawyer
4. Research comparable terms/standards
5. Prepare for negotiation

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Be specific** - "Draft an email to Sarah about rescheduling our Tuesday meeting to Thursday" works better than "send an email"

2. **Provide context** - "I'm preparing for a negotiation with a vendor" helps AI give relevant advice

3. **Iterate** - First draft not perfect? Ask AI to refine: "Make it more concise" or "Add more detail about X"

4. **Trust but verify** - AI drafts, you review. Especially for emails and external communications.

5. **Build habits** - Consistent daily use compounds. Morning inbox scan, evening capture, weekly review.
