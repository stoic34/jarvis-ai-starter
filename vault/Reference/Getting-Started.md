# Getting Started with Your AI Assistant

Welcome to your personal AI-powered knowledge management system.

## How It Works

1. **Obsidian** stores your notes, projects, and knowledge locally
2. **Claude Code** is your AI assistant that can read, write, and act on your behalf
3. **Tools** extend capabilities: email, calendar, voice transcription, document generation

## First Steps

If you haven't done the initial setup yet, tell your AI:

> "Let's do the initial setup"

This will walk you through naming your assistant and connecting your tools.

## Daily Workflow

### Morning
- Ask: "What's on my calendar today?"
- Ask: "Scan my inbox for anything urgent"
- Review `Daily/today.md` and set your focus

### Throughout the Day
- Capture quick thoughts: "Add to inbox: [your thought]"
- Draft emails: "Draft an email to [person] about [topic]"
- Research: "Research [topic] and summarize key points"

### Evening
- Process inbox: "Help me process my inbox"
- Capture learnings: "Add to today's notes: [what you learned]"

## Key Commands

| What You Want | What to Say |
|--------------|-------------|
| Check email | "Scan my inbox" |
| Draft email | "Draft an email to [person] about [topic]" |
| Check calendar | "What's on my calendar today/this week?" |
| Add a task | "Add to inbox: [task]" |
| Create a project | "Create a new project for [name]" |
| Transcribe audio | "Transcribe [file path]" |
| Create PDF | "Turn these notes into a PDF" |
| Research | "Research [topic]" |

## Getting Help

- Say: "What can you do?" for a list of capabilities
- Say: "Show me example prompts" for copy-paste starters
- Check `Reference/What-You-Can-Do.md` for detailed use cases

## Tips for Success

1. **Capture everything** - Your AI can help organize later
2. **Be specific** - "Email John about the meeting" vs "Send an email"
3. **Trust the process** - Let your AI draft, then review
4. **Iterate** - Your system will evolve as you use it
