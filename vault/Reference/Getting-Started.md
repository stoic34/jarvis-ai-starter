# Getting Started with Your AI Assistant

Welcome to your local AI assistant system.

## How It Works

1. **Obsidian** stores your notes, projects, and knowledge locally.
2. **Codex** is the recommended AI runtime on Mac.
3. **AGENTS.md** tells Codex and compatible agents how to behave.
4. **Tools** extend capabilities: email drafts, calendar context, voice transcription, and document generation.
5. **Optional remote Mac** lets you trigger work from your phone while a Mac does the execution.

## First Steps

If initial setup is not complete, tell your assistant:

> "Let's do the initial setup."

It should walk through naming, context files, tool checks, and first capture.

## Daily Workflow

### Morning

- Ask: "What's on my calendar today?"
- Ask: "Scan my inbox for anything urgent."
- Review `Daily/today.md`.

### Throughout the Day

- Capture: "Add to inbox: [your thought]."
- Draft: "Draft an email to [person] about [topic]."
- Research: "Research [topic] and summarize key points."

### Away From Your Laptop

- Send yourself a clear instruction from your phone.
- Let the remote Mac assistant process it.
- Review the result before anything external goes out.

### Evening

- Ask: "Help me process my inbox."
- Ask: "Summarize what changed today."
- Capture learnings in daily notes.

## Key Commands

| What You Want | What to Say |
|--------------|-------------|
| Check email | "Scan my inbox." |
| Draft email | "Draft an email to [person] about [topic]." |
| Check calendar | "What's on my calendar today?" |
| Add a task | "Add to inbox: [task]." |
| Create a project | "Create a new project for [name]." |
| Transcribe audio | "Transcribe [file path]." |
| Create PDF | "Turn these notes into a PDF." |
| Research | "Research [topic]." |
| Remote check | "Check whether the remote Mac is ready for Codex work." |

## Tips for Success

1. Capture first, organize later.
2. Use dictation for long instructions.
3. Let the assistant draft, then review.
4. Use Codex on Mac for desktop and browser workflows.
5. Keep `AGENTS.md` as the single instruction source.
