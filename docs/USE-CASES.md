# Real-World Use Cases

This document describes how people actually use their AI assistants day-to-day. These are based on real usage patterns, anonymized for sharing.

---

## Daily Operations

### Morning Routine
> "Scan my inbox and tell me what needs attention. Then show me my calendar for today."

The AI scans unread emails, categorizes by urgency/topic, and presents a summary. Combined with calendar view, gives a complete picture of the day ahead.

### Evening Capture
> "Here's what happened today: [voice note or typed summary]. Turn this into notes and extract any action items."

End-of-day brain dump processed into structured notes and tasks for tomorrow.

---

## Email Management

### Inbox Zero Assist
> "Help me process my inbox. For each email, tell me: respond, delegate, defer, or delete."

AI triages emails with recommendations. User makes final call, AI drafts responses.

### Complex Responses
> "Draft a response to [person] about [topic]. I need to [explain situation] while maintaining the relationship."

AI crafts nuanced professional communication, handling difficult conversations diplomatically.

### Follow-Up Tracking
> "What emails from last week haven't gotten responses? Draft follow-ups for the important ones."

AI tracks conversation threads and helps maintain momentum.

## Research & Analysis

### Deep Research Workflow
1. Run deep research using Perplexity or similar tool
2. Feed results to AI: "Here's research on [topic]. Summarize key insights and recommend actions."
3. AI synthesizes findings into actionable recommendations

### Competitive Analysis
> "Research [competitor/industry] and give me a SWOT analysis."

AI gathers information and structures it for strategic decision-making.

### Decision Support
> "I'm deciding between [options]. What are the pros and cons? What questions should I be asking?"

AI helps think through decisions systematically, often surfacing considerations the user hadn't thought of.

## Document Generation

### Reports and Summaries
> "Turn these notes into a one-page executive summary PDF."

AI transforms rough notes into polished, professional documents ready for sharing.

### Proposals and Pitches
> "Draft a proposal for [initiative]. Include: problem statement, proposed solution, timeline, and investment required."

AI creates structured business documents from high-level direction.

### Meeting Prep
> "I have a meeting about [topic] with [person]. Create a prep doc with: background, key points to cover, and questions to ask."

AI helps prepare for important conversations.

## Tutoring & Education

### Practice Test Generation
> "Create a 20-question practice test on [subject] at [grade level]. Include multiple choice, short answer, and problem-solving questions."

AI generates curriculum-aligned assessments with varied question types.

### Test Evaluation
> "Grade this test. For each wrong answer, explain the concept and where the student went wrong."

AI provides detailed feedback, identifying misconceptions and knowledge gaps.

### Adaptive Remediation
> "Based on the test results, create targeted practice exercises for the concepts that need work."

AI generates follow-up materials focused on weak areas, creating a personalized learning path.

### Study Guides
> "Create study notes for [topic] that a [grade level] student could understand. Include examples and memory tricks."

AI produces age-appropriate educational content with scaffolding for learning.

## Health & Wellness

### Supplement Review
> "Review this supplement stack for interactions and timing: [list supplements]"

AI checks for interactions, optimal timing, and potential redundancies. *Always discuss with healthcare provider.*

### Lab Work Analysis
> "Analyze these blood work results. What's outside optimal ranges (not just normal) and what might help?"

AI interprets results in context of optimization, not just disease prevention. *For discussion with doctor.*

### Protocol Design
> "Help me design a [sleep/exercise/nutrition] protocol for [goal]. Consider [constraints]."

AI creates structured protocols based on research and user-specific factors.

### Medical Research
> "Research the latest evidence on [health topic]. Focus on quality studies and practical implications."

AI synthesizes medical literature into actionable insights for informed discussions with providers.

## Legal & Contract Work

### Contract Review

AI provides structured framework for thorough review.

AI helps prepare negotiation strategy based on common patterns.

AI gathers relevant legal information for informed discussions with counsel.

With AWS backend:

> "Alert me if [condition] happens."

## Patterns That Work

### 1. Capture First, Process Later
Dump everything to inbox/voice memos. Let AI organize later when you have time.

### 2. Draft and Review
AI drafts, you review. Faster than starting from scratch, maintains quality control.

### 3. Research → Synthesize → Act
Use specialized research tools, feed to AI for synthesis, get actionable next steps.

### 4. Iterative Refinement
First draft not right? "Make it shorter." "More formal." "Add section on X." Iterate until done.

### 5. Daily Rituals
Consistent morning/evening AI interactions compound over time. Small habits, big impact.
